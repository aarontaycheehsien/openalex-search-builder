## Open bugs & improvements (prioritized)

### 0. Done (2026-11-26) — fixes from the blind benchmark runs
- **Scope re-confirmation friction:** the scope decision now covers blocks, roles, protocol, limits, corpus and pulled setting, not rationale wording, so editing a rationale no longer blocks `eval` (it still changes the evaluation fingerprint). A refused `eval` names what changed and the command to run. Scopes confirmed under the old hash stay valid. (In nearly all ten blind runs the agent was refused after a rationale edit.)
- **Stale figures in critiques:** `review` fills development-only placeholders (`{final_count}`, `{development_recall}`, `{pulled_count}` and others) from the current evaluation, records them in the review and the audit, and refuses a critique that quotes an earlier evaluation's final count but not the current one. (Three rerun critiques quoted stale development, validation or pulled counts.)
- **Source reviews surfacing in discovery:** `neighbors` and `sample` apply the strategy's publication-date limits to candidates (`--any-date` turns this off). All three possible source documents seen in the blind runs were published after the cutoff; live, the window removed the Kapuka 2021 source review from the related works of W2808836827 (9 works to 4).

- **Broadening needs evidence:** `eval` reports `revision` (count change, development works gained and lost, works per gained development work) and warns `broadening_without_gain` when the search grows by more than 20% and 500 works without gaining a development work; `sample --added` samples only the records the latest revision added; `eval` and `report` estimate precision from screened random samples of the exact current search (strict and lenient, 95% Wilson intervals, estimated eligible records) and `audit.md` reports it; SKILL.md requires evidence for keeping a broadening. Live: adding "telehealth" to a 1,140-work search added 922 works and raised the warning; `sample --added` drew 25 records, 14 of them new.
- **Citation-link coverage:** `neighbors` refuses a seed with no matched references or citations in the chosen direction before any paid request, `--direction related` is capped at OpenAlex's 10 and labelled a text-similarity route, and PRISMA-S states link coverage and the 25 November 2026 link change.

### 1. Medium — `rescue` timeouts on large strategies (cited-by solved; citing still open)
- **Observed:** On `Lewowski_2021` (~180 leaves), `rescue --direction both` failed with HTTP 400 `query_too_slow` because `both` embeds the full Boolean query twice inside `set (works where ...)`, whereas `--direction cited-by` succeeded in seconds and rescued 1/1 abstract-less miss. On `Moran_2020` (238 leaves), even single-direction subquery materialization timed out.
- **Also observed (2026-11-25):** with or without an API key, rescue intermittently fails with HTTP 500 `es_error` "Elasticsearch error: Connection timed out", even on the small `examples/telehealth.json` strategy and on `cited-by` alone. Failures are erratic, not size-driven (a 47k-work set failed while a 63k-work set succeeded), and OpenAlex's own pre-check reported these queries `within_budget` (6–9 s of 10 s), so `check.estimate` does not predict them. `api.request` does not retry HTTP 500.
- **Done (see Implemented):** retry of HTTP 500 `es_error` timeouts with 2/4/8 s backoff (`query_too_slow` refusals are not retried); warning when OpenAlex's estimate is within 80% of its time budget; default `--direction cited-by`; `both` run as two single-direction queries, deduplicated on export; actionable advice when a direction still times out.
- **Live retest (2026-11-25):** `examples/telehealth.json` (29,673 works) now completes in both directions. `examples/telehealth-advanced.json` (40,622 works, all corpora, proximity, exclusion) still times out on every attempt, with or without `--topic`: OpenAlex's estimate stays at 8.0 s of 10 s, so the cost is rebuilding the delivered set, not the outer gate.
- **Export of rescue records:** paging a citation-set query re-runs it for every 100-record page, and for searches of about 30,000 works every paging attempt timed out (2026-11-25). Solved for `cited-by`, whose reference-list rescue is an ID list exported by ID lookups; still open for `citing`, which only has the citation-set query.
- **Done (2026-11-26) — reference-list rescue for `cited-by`:** `rescue --method references` (the default for cited-by) reads the delivered works' matched reference lists from the primary export, or pages the delivered search for them, keeps cited works outside the delivered set without an abstract inside the date window (ID lookups, 1 credit per 100), and applies the gate to those IDs (100 per request). The result is a dated ID list; `export --rescue` fetches it by ID and `bench.py score --with-rescue` scores it directly. `rescue --estimate` gives the cost first. Live on 1,140 delivered works it returned 612 works: exactly the citation-set query's 641 minus 29 works already in the delivered set. It also saves the development works' unlinked reference lines (free) as leads for other sources. Not covered: the `citing` direction, which still uses the citation-set query.
- **Still open:** a live retest on `Lewowski_2021` and `Moran_2020`.

### 2. Done — Stemming & single-token hyphen quote-stripping hazards
- **Observed:** In `Moran_2020`, stemming and quoted single-token hyphenated phrases (`"open-field"`, `"risk-prone"`, `"anti-predator"`) inflated initial hits to 42,154 before fixing brought them to 5,969.
- **Verified live (2026-11-25):** OpenAlex keeps a quoted hyphenated term on the exact field but runs it as an AND of its parts anywhere, not as a phrase: `"open-field"` 10,487 titles = `"open" AND "field"` 10,487, while the phrase `"open field"` gives 4,872 and also matches hyphenated titles. `"risk-prone"` 958 against 131; `"COVID-19"` is unaffected (968,014 against 967,735). `stemmed "risk-prone"` is a phrase (158 = `stemmed "risk prone"`). Stemming collisions are word-specific: `fasting` 471,555 stemmed against 33,935 exact (it stems to `fast`), but `ration` does not collide with `rational` (16,830 stemmed; `rational` alone 113,619). The translation guard cannot see these, because the term and its words survive translation.
- **Done:** `lint` warning `hyphenated_phrase` suggesting the spaced phrase; clearer `unquoted_hyphen` message; lint warnings returned as `strategy_warnings` by `validate` and `eval`; new `term-counts [--block ID]` command that counts each term alone (with the strategy's corpus, limits and pulled view) and flags `stemming_expands` (stemmed ≥ 3× exact and ≥ 100 more works) and `hyphen_not_phrase` (≥ 1.5× the spaced phrase and ≥ 50 more); guidance in `SKILL.md` step 5.

### 3. Medium — Stronger vocabulary & discovery tools
- **Enrichment-ranked `terms`:** Rank mined terms by frequency in development records vs. a background sample rather than raw document frequency (the objective approach of Hausner et al., 2012, https://doi.org/10.1186/2046-4053-1-19). Worth it mainly at `thorough` depth: with two or three seeds neither raw counts nor lift say much.
- **Done — Multi-seed `neighbors`:** `neighbors` accepts multiple development Work IDs in one deduplicated snowballing batch, ranking co-linked candidates by `seed_count` first (excluding seeds and protected records).
- **Post-export abstract enrichment (for screening):** Optional enrichment of missing abstracts in `results.csv` / `results.ris` via PubMed / Europe PMC / Crossref by DOI/PMID, with provenance logged in `export-manifest.json`.

A relaxation that leaves no search block is listed in `ablation_notes`. The tool fills what its record supports (database and platform, corpus, strategy as run, limits, pulled view, date, total, development citation searching) and marks the rest for the reviewer.

### 7. Done — Small fixes
- The audit's revision history is ordered by each evaluation's stored `at` (file time only breaks ties), so copying or cloning a workspace no longer reorders it.
- `check()` refuses, offline, the wildcard forms OpenAlex rejects (verified 2026-11-25): fewer than three leading characters, also inside phrases (`"te* care"`), and wildcards in a stemmed phrase. `lint` and `draft` now report these as errors rather than warnings.
- Readable errors: a missing step names the command to run (`Run eval first.`, `Attach a critique with the review command first.`), a corrupted workspace file names the file and position, and a missing field names the command and field instead of printing a bare `KeyError`.
- `resolve` accepts PMCIDs (`PMC123`, `pmcid:123`, PMC article URLs), normalised to `pmcid:PMC123`; bare digits stay PMIDs.
- RIS export adds `SN` (each ISSN), `PB` (publisher) and `KW` (OpenAlex keywords); the CSV gains `issn`, `publisher` and `keywords`. Author names stay as OpenAlex displays them: reversing them to `Last, First` would guess wrongly for many names, and reference managers parse either order.

### 8. Open — Recall estimation and efficiency (suggested 2026-11-26)
- **Done — `eval --quick`:** skips the ablation lines (`without/`, `drop-route/`, `with/`) to save requests while developing; the block, exclusion and limit lines stay because miss explanations depend on them. `review` requires a full `eval`, since the critique must see the ablation and `report` re-runs a full one.
- **Done (2026-11-26) — capture–recapture estimate:** `eval`, `report` (`audit.md`, `validation.json`) and `bench.py score` give Chapman's (1951) estimate of the number of eligible works, with the variance usually attributed to Seber (1970, https://doi.org/10.2307/2529040), from the overlap of eligible works found by text routes (keyword samples, related works) and citation routes, and the share discovery reached (Spoor et al., 1996, https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.313.7053.342; Kastner et al., 2009, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2008.06.001). Discovery now records validation works it hides, so they still count as recaptures. Computed for the ten blind runs, the routes overlapped by only 0–5 works and discovery reached 20–50% of the estimate; the estimate could not be checked against gold because the agents' eligible works were mostly not the reviews' included studies (0–63%, median 6%). See RESULTS.md.
- **Demoted (2026-11-26) — capture–recapture is a diagnostic only:** in the ten blind runs discovery completeness tracked gold recall only weakly (Spearman 0.31, n = 10), the 95% intervals spanned 1.1–2 times the estimate, and two runs on the same review differed up to twofold. It now reports an estimate only with at least 20 eligible works from each route and 10 found by both ("not estimable" otherwise, which applied to all ten runs), is no longer in `audit.md` or SKILL.md's advice, and stays in `eval` (compact), `validation.json` and `bench.py score`. Re-test in seeded blind runs before giving it a reporting role.
- **SKILL.md trimmed for context length (2026-11-26):** 3,173 to 2,360 words; benchmark figures and dates moved out (they are in RESULTS.md), long steps split into bullets, no instruction dropped.
- **Done (2026-11-26) — eligibility calibration check:** `bench.py score` now reports, for the evaluator only, how many gold works discovery surfaced, how the agent decided on those it screened, and how many agent-eligible works are gold. The premise turned out wrong: across the ten blind runs the agents included 36 of the 40 gold works they screened, marked 4 unclear and excluded none, so screening is not too lenient (the low share of agent-eligible works that are gold can reflect incomplete gold sets). The strict-screening sentence briefly added to SKILL.md was replaced with guidance to widen discovery.
- **Discovery reach (2026-11-26, partly done):** discovery surfaced only 0–14 of each review's 26–28 included studies. Analysis of the ten blind runs (RESULTS.md): included studies are 0.1–1.6% of the final searches, so random samples rarely hold one; in four of five reviews they seldom cite each other (6–9 of 26–28 linked), so one citation step from the development works reached only 0–7; the top 200 by relevance held about 4–6 times what random samples would. Done: `sample --top` (best matches first, kept out of the precision estimate) and SKILL.md guidance to ask for known eligible studies and screen ranked samples. Still open: blind runs in the seeded condition, to test whether reviewer-supplied seeds lift recall and make development figures meaningful; and stratified samples (by year or topic) as a further discovery route.
- **Multi-seed ranking note:** candidates are ranked by how many seeds link to them; with about 18% of reference links missing (https://help.openalex.org/data/works/citations/) that count understates shared citations. State this in the `neighbors` help text.

### 9. Done (2026-11-27) — Batch 3 operational fixes, precision/recall CLI levers, `doctor` & 12-fixture `gold-profile`
- **Operational fixes from Batch 3:** raised `API.members` default `batch_size` from `25` to `100`; added workspace `query-cache.json` across `eval` rounds (with `--no-cache` override, while `report` always re-executes live uncached); added `delivery_estimate` (`estimated_credits`, `estimated_usd`) to `eval` output so agents reserve budget for `report`; made `{without_abstract_pct}` fill `"not measured (eval ran without --abstract-stats)"` instead of failing `review`; added `oasb.py doctor [--offline]` and CLI `.env` auto-loading fallback.

Still to run when daily credits reset: `replay.py impact` and `replay.py rescue` on archived deliveries plus blind A/B runs of the staged `SKILL.md` branch.
